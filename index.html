Controls

Click a slot, then press a key or a controller button. A keyboard key can only do one job, so taking a key moves it here. Press Esc to cancel.

Items

Press Attack near an item to pick it up, then Attack to use or throw it. Down + Attack drops it or sets a trap, and Dodge throws whatever you’re holding. Food and power-ups work as soon as you touch them.
